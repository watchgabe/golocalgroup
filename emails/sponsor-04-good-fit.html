A Program Built for Local Businesses

Our partnership program was designed to be straightforward, accessible, and valuable for local companies. It provides consistent visibility within a community of more than 1,000 active families while supporting athletes working toward regional, national, and Olympic-level competition.

Partners Receive

▶ Year-round brand visibility inside the facility
▶ Exposure to a highly engaged community of families and athletes
▶ Digital promotion across social media, email, and the Texas Ford Aquatics website
▶ Opportunities to connect with families during swim meets and events
▶ Alignment with a program known for discipline, performance, and athlete development

Why It Works

For many businesses, the program works well because it offers meaningful exposure in a trusted environment while remaining a relatively low-risk marketing investment.
More importantly, it connects your brand with a community that values hard work, commitment, and long-term success.
